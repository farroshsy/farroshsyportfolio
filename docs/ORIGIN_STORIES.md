# Origin stories — status

**Both are written and live** as the opening section of each case study
(`Why I built it`, in `src/content/projects.ts`). They were assembled from
first-person source material: Bab I of the KP report for the OCR project, and
Bab I / Bab III of the TA book for HCIE.

Nothing in them is invented. Every fact traces to one of those documents.

## The one sentence still missing from each

Both explain the **structural** reason convincingly. Neither answers why *you*
took it on rather than someone else. One sentence, no grandeur required, added
where marked:

- **HCIE** — after "…the population it was meant to serve." Why this problem and
  not another.
- **doc-verify** — after "…an incident or a compliance finding." What made you
  pick this out of everything else you saw during the internship.

Optional. The stories work without it; they would work slightly better with it,
because a structural reason explains the project and a personal one explains the
person.

## Where they also belong

1. Top of each public README, above the feature list — it is the first
   paragraph a hiring manager reads in their 40-second pass. **Not yet done.**

## On language

If you rewrite either, write it in Indonesian first and translate after.
Motivation is the hardest thing to write in a second language, and it is the one
place where stiffness reads as insincerity rather than accent.
